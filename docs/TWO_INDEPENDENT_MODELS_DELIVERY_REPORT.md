## 1. Executive Summary

The Local AI Companion system in `D:\DEEP-LEARNING-PROJECT` has been architected, deployed, and verified with **Two Completely Independent AI Models**:

- **Zero Automatic Switching**: The chatbot never switches models automatically based on prompt complexity or intent.
- **Zero Silent Fallback**: If a model fails or is offline, it never secretly delegates to the other model.
- **Strict Project Isolation**: 100% of files, runtimes, models, and virtual environments reside inside `D:\DEEP-LEARNING-PROJECT`. No system-wide installs, no global registry changes, and no Ollama dependency.

| `/exit` | Safely closes chatbot sessions, stops background servers, and unloads GPU/RAM buffers. |
